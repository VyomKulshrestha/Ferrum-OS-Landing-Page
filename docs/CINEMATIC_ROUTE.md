# FerrumOS cinematic route

The landing page is one continuous camera journey, but continuity does not mean repeating one corridor. The opening tunnel is limited to the boot and privilege-boundary transition. Every later chapter must reveal a materially different place, scale, and direction of travel.

## World route

1. **Forge** — wake a black-iron compute wafer and enter its boot aperture.
2. **Boundary** — cross the concentric privilege rings and emerge into a system city.
3. **Userland** — travel through distinct process, storage, network, and audio districts, then descend into an authority foundry.
4. **Authority** — cross five stacked permission levels at the bottom of the foundry, then rise vertically toward open sky.
5. **World model** — break through storm clouds into a dual-forecast observatory where deterministic and learned futures meet at one cautious gate.
6. **Evidence** — follow the accepted forecast down through data rain into a deep, inspectable evidence archive where misses stay visible.
7. **Inputs** — leave the archive for two bounded laboratories: physical simulation in shadow mode and signed neural intent stopped before actuator authority.
8. **Open horizon** — emerge into a wide exterior landscape whose source, dataset, report, and contribution paths converge at dawn.

## Continuity contract

- Each new shot starts from the previous shot's lossless final PNG.
- Forward motion must materially advance into a new environment; a loop or return to the prior composition is rejected.
- From Scene 3 onward, no shot may collapse into another generic tunnel.
- Direction changes are motivated by the world: descend into the foundry, rise through the weather layer, descend into the archive, then emerge outdoors.
- The last second keeps a slow, centered drift so the following shot has a stable handoff.

## Quality gate

- Preserve deep blacks, crisp iron micro-detail, ember orange, cold cyan, and restrained violet.
- Reject gray haze, soft surfaces, flat contrast, desaturation, overexposure, camera shake, or spatial discontinuity.
- Text and measured claims remain semantic HTML overlays, never generated into the video.
- Raw generation downloads remain local and ignored. Only optimized silent MP4 delivery files and approved lossless handoff frames are versioned.
- Every shipped clip is decoded, inspected at its midpoint and final frame, and covered by the media-size regression contract.
