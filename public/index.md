# FerrumOS

FerrumOS is a bootable Rust x86-64 research operating system where an AI agent runs in Ring 3 and every effect remains subject to kernel-enforced authority.

## Claim boundary

FerrumOS v0.1.1 is the latest tagged software release. Current main contains newer research. FerrumOS is not a production-ready general-purpose OS or certified safety platform. Physical results are simulator-only, neural results are synthetic or recorded evidence only, and no live-EEG, medical, real-robot, or broad physical-PC claim is made.
